# SURF-03 · Draws written

**A passage to write; it has no key.**

**Story.** RAND-01's lifetime: a life table from 85 (0.2, 0.3, 0.5, 1), a lifetime drawn year by year, seed 20251024, 60 000 paths, the mean reported with its standard error.

**Write:** the draw, the path it lives on, and the plan over paths (seed, count, what is reported), as a reader would meet them in a household model.

**What the passage shows:** how a draw is written apart from a fixed value; where the seed and the path count live; that a statistic across paths is written outside any one path.
