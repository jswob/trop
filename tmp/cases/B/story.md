A flat bought in 2022 in shares, on a mortgage; a business car bought on credit and sold; a client who pays in florens; a rule on the 26th that sends spare money to the mortgage and to savings. The entries run from June 2019 to 7 January 2026. Each entry: `date · known <date> · <document or record>`; a fact without a document is stated in our own words.
