# The Lenia–Rosvald Agreement on Taxes and Social Security

Signed at Ostrel on 2024-05-14. Part I is a treaty on taxes on income, Part II coordinates social security and family benefits, Part III says when each part takes effect; the protocol is part of it. In the home law's format (`tmp/cases/law/acts.md` § How to read it): the articles are `T-n`, each with the points it holds. PIT-16 .2 of the home law (`law/home-supplement.md`) makes Part I apply in Lenia's tax; FAM-1 .6 and RFF-1 .2 make Part II apply to the child benefits.

## Part I · Taxes on income

### T-2 · Residence
1. A person is a resident of a state where its law taxes them on their income from everywhere.
2. A person who is a resident of both states is a resident only of the state where they have a permanent home; if they have one in both, of the state where their personal and economic ties are closer.

### T-5 · Pay for work
1. Pay a resident of one state gets for work done in the other state may be taxed in the state where the work is done.
2. Pay for work done in the state of residence is taxed only there.

### T-7 · Relief from double tax, in Lenia
1. Where a resident of Lenia gets pay that T-5 .1 lets Rosvald tax, Lenia exempts it from its tax.
2. Lenia may take the exempt pay into account in computing the tax on the person's other income (exemption with progression).

### T-8 · Relief from double tax, in Rosvald
1. Rosvald's law applies to pay for work done in Rosvald; Rosvald gives no relief for Lenia's tax on it.

## Part II · Social security and family benefits

### T-10 · Insurance
1. A person who works in one state is insured under that state's law only, for the months of that work.

### T-11 · Working in a state
1. A parent works in a state in a month when, for that month, they are paid for work done in that state, or are paid parental pay under that state's law. A month of unpaid leave is not a month of work.

### T-12 · Family benefits
1. Where, for the same child and the same month, a family benefit is due under the law of both states:
   (a) where a parent works in the state where the child lives, that state pays its benefit first, and the other state pays the difference between its own benefit and the first, where its own is higher;
   (b) otherwise, the state where a parent works pays first, and the state where the child lives pays the difference, where its own benefit is higher.
2. For the difference, the first state's benefit is converted into the currency of the state paying the difference, at that state's central bank's rate last published on or before the first day of the month the benefit is for — for Rosvald the RNB's reference rate (`RNB-DUK`), for Lenia the CBL's mid rate (`FX-FLR`) — and rounded to the cent (the ort), half up.
3. Each state pays at the times its own law sets.

### T-13 · Changes
1. A change in which state pays first applies from the month in which it happens.
2. A state that paid what this Part did not give recovers it under its own law.

## Part III · Taking effect

### T-20 · Entry into force
1. The agreement enters into force on the thirtieth day after the later of the two notes saying it has been ratified. (The notes were exchanged on 2024-10-29; it entered into force on 2024-11-28.)
2. Part I has effect for taxes on income derived on or after 1 January of the calendar year after the year in which it entered into force. Part II has effect from the first day of the month after it entered into force.

## Protocol

1. On T-7: Rosvald's wage tax on pay that T-7 exempts is not deducted from Lenia's tax.
2. On T-20 .2: until Part I has effect, each state taxes under its own law alone.
3. On T-12: a benefit a state paid before Part II had effect stays paid.
