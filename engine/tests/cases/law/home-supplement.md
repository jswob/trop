# The home law: what the visible copy leaves out

Part of the law of the Republic of Lenia (`tmp/cases/law/acts.md`), in its format. Only the hidden household reads these, so the visible copy leaves them out; in the hidden set the home law is that copy together with this file. A point given here reads in place of the visible copy's point with the same number, or is added to its rule. Moved here from `acts.md` on 2026-10-24, before any household was written; there `PIT-12`–`PIT-14` were `PIT-13`–`PIT-15`. Since step 6, the visible `PIT-15` is household B's business revenue, so the foreign-income rule here is `PIT-16`.

## Points that read in place of the visible copy's

- **PIT-5 v1 .3** (and so v2 .3, which reads "as v1"): The tax is the PIT-3 tax on that base (the version governing the tax year), less the child relief (PIT-7), less the year's health contributions to the extent of 7.75 % of their bases (SOC-2 v1 .2), never below 0; then less the foreign tax credit (PIT-16); rounded (GEN-5.1).
- **PIT-6 v1 .4**: Both spouses' child reliefs and foreign tax credits (PIT-16) are deducted from the joint tax, never below 0, and both spouses' advances are deducted from the result.
- **SOC-3 v1 .5**: In the month the business starts or closes, the base is pro-rated: base times the days of activity in the month over the month's days.

## Points added to visible rules

- **PIT-10 v1 .7**: For a business closed during a year, the period of point 3 ends on the closing day: the year's profit counts only the invoices dated up to that day and the contributions paid up to it.
- **FAM-1 v1 .6 and v2 .6**: Where another state's law gives a family benefit for the same child for the same month, and an agreement between Lenia and that state coordinates family benefits for that month, this benefit is paid only as the agreement provides.
- **VAT-4 v1 .4**: On closing the business, the return for the month of closing is the last.
- **IGT-2 v1 .4**: Releasing a debt is a gift of the amount released, performed on the day of the release. Where the debt is owed by the spouses' community property to one spouse, the release is a gift to the other spouse of half the amount released.

## Rules added

### PIT-16 · Foreign income and foreign tax
**v1** · in force 2018-01-01 – until replaced · published 2017-09-01 · governs: tax years from 2018
1. Income from abroad is converted under GEN-4; the event is the day it is paid, for pay, and the invoice's issue day, for business revenue.
2. Where a treaty between Lenia and the state of source says how that income is taxed, the treaty applies.
3. Otherwise the tax paid abroad on that income is deducted from the tax (PIT-5.3) up to the part of the tax that falls on that income: the tax times that income's base over the whole base.

### PIT-17 · Gifts to public-benefit bodies
**v1** · in force 2018-01-01 – until replaced · published 2017-09-01 · governs: tax years from 2018
1. A gift of money to a body on the RO's list of public-benefit bodies, paid in the tax year by transfer from a payment account, is deducted from the giver's base (PIT-5.2) for that year.
2. A gift paid from the spouses' community property is a gift of each spouse for half, whichever of them ordered it.
3. Each giver's deduction is at most 10 % of that giver's base before the deduction. What is above it is not deducted, in that year or any other.
4. The deduction is carried exactly. The parents' income under PIT-7.2 is taken before it.

### PIT-18 · Pay from work abroad
**v1** · in force 2018-01-01 – until replaced · published 2017-09-01 · governs: tax years from 2018
1. For pay from an employment abroad, the income is each payment's gross converted under PIT-16 .1; its base is that income, less the foreign social contributions withheld from it converted the same way, less the costs of PIT-2.2 for each month in which such pay is paid, and is never below 0.
2. The foreign tax withheld from each payment is converted the same way.
3. Where the foreign state refunds tax on a tax year of its own, the refund is shared among that year's payments in proportion to their gross, each share to the cent, half up; each share reduces the foreign tax on its payment, converted under GEN-4 with the day the refund is received as the event. Where a return for a year with such a payment was already filed, the taxpayer files a corrected return within 1 month of that day, and pays any further tax by its deadline.
4. Where a treaty exempts the pay with progression, the tax (PIT-5.3; under PIT-6.3, the joint tax) is the tax computed on the base plus the exempt base, times the base over that sum; the exempt base is the sum of the exempt payments' bases under point 1, rounded (GEN-5.1). The exempt pay is in no base of PIT-5.2, PIT-7.2 or PIT-17 .3.
5. In the credit of PIT-16 .3: the tax is the tax after the child relief; in a joint return it is the joint tax and the whole base is the combined base, after PIT-17. The income's base is the sum of the credited payments' bases under point 1. The credit is rounded (GEN-5.1); what it does not deduct is lost.

### FAM-2 · Benefits paid without entitlement
**v1** · in force 2018-01-01 – until replaced · published 2017-09-01 · governs: benefits paid from 2018-01-01
1. A parent who gets a family benefit reports to the FBO, within 14 days, any change that alters the entitlement, another state's benefit included.
2. A benefit paid for a month for which it was not due is repaid. The FBO sets the amount by a decision, and may set monthly instalments, at most 12; repaid on the decision's terms, it bears no interest.
