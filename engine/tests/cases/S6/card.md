## Law

### MRG-1 · Spouses' property
**v1** · in force 2005-01-01 – until replaced · published 2004-05-20 · governs: every marriage, from 2005-01-01
1. Pay either spouse receives while married is joint, and so is what is bought with joint money. What a spouse had before marrying, inherits or is given is their own.
2. Money leaves an account in the order it came in: each payment takes the oldest money still there, whoever owns it and whatever the payment is for. Money that came in on one day is ordered as the statement lists it; the money in the account when the statement starts is older than any of its rows. No claim between the spouses arises from a payment.

### DIV-1 · Divorce
**v1** · in force 2005-01-01 – until replaced · published 2004-05-20 · governs: divorces from 2005-01-01
1. Joint property ends on the day the divorce judgment becomes final. From that day each former spouse owns half of every former joint thing and of the former joint money; pay either receives from that day is their own.
2. Former spouses may divide the former joint property by a written agreement; from its signing each alone owns what it gives them, and the payments it sets are due as it says.

## House rules

### H-1 · What we are worth
**v1** · from 2013-09-07
1. Each of us is worth their own money and things, plus half of the joint money and things (after the divorce, of the former joint ones until they are divided), less what they owe. No rounding: half an ort stays half an ort.
2. The car counts at its latest valuation on or before the day.
