## Law

### PRP-1 · Things bought together
**v1** · in force 2010-01-01 – until replaced · published 2009-08-14 · governs: things acquired from 2010-01-01
1. People who are not married each own what they acquire alone; they may agree in writing how money they hold together is owned and spent.
2. A thing bought with several people's money belongs to them in shares of what each paid toward its price and the costs of buying it, unless they agreed other shares in writing. A rise or fall in its value falls on them in those shares.
