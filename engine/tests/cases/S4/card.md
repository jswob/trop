## Law

### SPS-1 · Spouses' property
**v1** · in force 1975-01-01 – until replaced · published 1974-06-03 · governs: every marriage, from 1975-01-01
1. Pay and pensions received while married are joint, in whatever account, as is what joint money buys; what a spouse had before marrying, inherits or is given is their own.
2. At a death, half of each joint thing and of the joint money in each account becomes the survivor's own, half the estate's; what the survivor receives afterwards is their own.

### SUC-1 · Heirs and the estate
**v1** · in force 1990-01-01 – 2027-06-30 · published 1989-05-02 · governs: deaths up to 2027-06-30
1. The will names the heirs and shares (with no will: spouse and children, equally). From the death they own the estate together, each in their share of every thing, sum and debt in it.
2. The funeral is an estate debt; an estate debt paid from an account also holding other money takes the estate's money first.
3. A notary's deed may divide the estate: from its day each heir alone owns what it gives them; its payments fall due as it says.
4. A child the will leaves nothing may claim from the heirs half their no-will share of the estate's net value at the death (sworn appraiser's values for that day, plus money, less debts, the funeral included), rounded to whole orts, half up; the claim arises at the death.
5. The heirs owe it in their shares: the first-named heir's part rounded to whole orts, half up, the other's the rest.
6. No claim if the reason the will gives for leaving the child out is true; a court decides a dispute about it.

**v2** · in force 2027-07-01 – until replaced · published 2027-05-14 · governs: deaths from 2027-07-01 · As v1, but point 4 gives two thirds instead of half.

## House rules

### H-1 · What each of us is worth
**v1** · from 2020-01-01
1. What one owns less what one owes: joint property half to each spouse, a share at that share of the value, a claim for the one owed, against the one owing. No rounding: half an ort stays.
2. A flat or house counts at its latest valuation for a day on or before the day asked; money at its balance.
