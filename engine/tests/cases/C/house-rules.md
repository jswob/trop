# Household C · our house rules

Marta's and Jan's rules, in their words, as both signed them. Each says when it was signed; C-HR1 says when that makes it start. "We" and "our" are the two of us; "business day" is Lenia's (GEN-1) unless a rule says otherwise.

## C-HR1 · When a rule starts

Signed by both on 2024-06-20.
1. A rule, or a new version of one, starts on the first day of the month after the month in which the second of us signs it.
2. A rule may name an earlier start. It then reaches back to that start, but never to a month that a settle-up (C-HR3 .5) has already closed: it starts at the first month no settle-up has closed.

## C-HR2 · Jan's florens

v1: signed by Jan on 2024-06-20, by Marta on 2024-06-24.
1. Jan pays his part (C-HR3) by selling florens at Bank Lazur, for exactly the dukats due, into the joint account.
2. When Bank Lazur's buy rate for the floren was at or above 4.1100 on every day of a month on which it published one, Jan sells all his florens above 1 500.00 on the first business day of the next month, into the pot. There is never more than one such sale in a calendar quarter; sales under point 3 don't count.
3. Otherwise, when Jan holds more than 14 000.00 florens at a month's end, he sells those above 6 000.00 on the first business day of the next month, into the pot.
4. A sale is at Bank Lazur's buy rate of the day it is made.

v2: signed by Jan on 2026-09-22, by Marta on 2026-09-25. As v1, but from 2026-10-01 to 2027-09-30 points 2 and 3 don't apply: Jan keeps his florens, apart from his part.

## C-HR3 · Jan's part of the house

v1: signed by Jan on 2024-06-20, by Marta on 2024-06-24.
1. For each month from July 2024, Jan's part is half of the month's house costs, times the nights he slept at home that month over the month's nights, to the ort, half up.
2. The house costs of a month: the mortgage instalment paid in it, the water, electricity and gas bills paid from the joint account in it, and 2 400.00 for food.
3. A night at home is a night Jan's log marks "dom".
4. He pays it by the 10th of the next month (the next business day where the 10th isn't one).
5. Settle-up: on the last day of February and of August we compare what Jan owed for the six months before the settle-up's month with what he paid for them. The difference is paid within 7 days. A settle-up closes those six months.

v2: signed by Jan on 2025-03-20, by Marta on 2025-04-02; it names its start: "from January 2025". As v1, but point 2: the mortgage instalment, the water, electricity, gas and internet bills paid from the joint account in the month, and 2 800.00 for food.

## C-HR4 · Big purchases

Signed by Marta on 2024-12-30, by Jan on 2025-01-03.
1. A purchase of more than 2 500.00 dukats from our money needs the other's yes. One purchase is one thing bought, however it is paid.
2. Ask by message. "Tak" is yes, "nie" is no. No answer within 7 days of the ask is a yes — but silence is never a yes in December.
3. A purchase in florens counts in dukats.
4. A purchase made against this rule is the buyer's own: the buyer pays it back into the joint account at the next settle-up (C-HR3 .5).

## C-HR5 · Staś's money

Signed by both on 2023-04-01; it names its start: "from 2023-04-03".
1. What Irena sends "dla Stasia" is Staś's. It stays in the joint account.
2. We spend it last: at each day's end the joint account holds Staś's money first, up to all he has been sent. When the balance is below that, the shortfall is ours to put back, and the next money in fills it first.
3. On 2024-01-01 all he had been sent was there.

## C-HR6 · The tenth

Signed by both on 2023-11-30.
1. Each December we give the year's tenth to Fundacja Pod Dębem, by transfer from the joint account.
2. A year's tenth is the smallest whole-dukat amount that is at least a tenth of: both our bases on that year's return before the gift is deducted, less the tax due that the return would show with that amount as the gift.
3. In December the year's return isn't filed, so we give our estimate of the year's tenth, with what the year before carries added or taken off. Next December's gift adds what the estimate was too low, or takes off what it was too high, measured by the year's return as last filed before that gift; what the estimate was is what we gave, less what the year before added or plus what it took off.

## C-HR7 · Marta's loan

Signed by both on 2025-06-20; it names its start: "from today". It is the loan agreement (C-D31).
1. Marta lends our community 30 000.00 from her own money, paid out on 2025-06-20.
2. Interest runs from the day it is paid out: each day, CBL-REF in force that day, over 365, of what we owe at the start of the day (on the first day, the 30 000.00). It is added to what we owe at each month's end, and on the loan's last day, to the ort, half up.
3. We repay 1 000.00 from the joint account on the 10th of each month from August 2025 (the next business day where the 10th isn't one).
4. At the start of our tenth wedding anniversary, 2026-09-17, Marta forgives whatever we then owe. The loan's last day is 2026-09-16.

## C-HR8 · The extra

Signed by both on 2025-06-20; it names its start: "from July 2025".
1. On the 16th of each month (the next business day where it isn't one), 800.00 goes from the pot to the mortgage as an overpayment.
2. Except: a month's 800.00 is skipped when our plan shows the pot below 5 000.00 at the end of any day in the 12 months after that month's 16th, with that month's 800.00 paid.
3. Our plan is our forecast of the pot with the 800.00 of each later month that this rule pays, and without those it skips.

## C-HR9 · How we count what we have

Signed by both on 2025-01-03.
1. Florens count at Bank Lazur's buy rate of the day, each amount of florens on its own, to the ort, half up.
2. What is ours together (CIV-1) counts half for each of us, the half carried exactly; what is one's own counts for that one.
3. Money owed to us counts once it is set or claimed (a decision, a claim filed, an agreement); money we owe, once it is set.
4. The house and the car count at a value only once they are valued. Neither has been, so what we have leaves them out and names them: not a nought, a gap.
5. The mortgage counts at the principal owed.
6. Staś's money is his, not ours.
7. What one of us owes the joint account under C-HR3 or C-HR4 isn't counted, nor what the joint account owes one of us under them.
