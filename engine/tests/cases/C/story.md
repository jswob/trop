# Household C · Marta and Jan

Marta and Jan Wrona live in their own house, ul. Graniczna 12, Zabór, a town in Lenia about 40 km from the border with Rosvald, with their son Staś. Until June 2024 Jan ran a joinery workshop in Zabór; from July 2024 he works across the border, in Ostrel, and is paid in florens. Both have lived in Lenia all their lives, and the house in Zabór is their only home. The dossier runs from 2024-01-01 to 2026-09-30.
