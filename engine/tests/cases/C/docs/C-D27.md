- issuer: Bank Jodła S.A.

Borrowers: Jan Wrona and Marta Wrona. Loan HIP/2019/04471: 400 000.00 DUK, paid out 2019-05-20, for the house ul. Graniczna 12, Zabór. Fixed rate 6.20 % a year for the whole term.

1. 300 monthly instalments of 2 626.33 DUK from 2019-06-15, on the 15th of each month, or the next business day where the 15th isn't one, debited from the joint account.
2. Each instalment's interest is 1/12 of 6.20 % of the principal owed at the end of the day before it, to the ort, half up; the rest of the instalment repays principal. The last instalment is what is then owed.
3. Overpayments may be made on any day, without fee; they reduce the principal on their day and shorten the term; the instalment stays the same.
