# Household A · our house rules

Our own rules, in our words. Each has the date it starts and, where it changed, its versions.

## A-HR1 · The 15th: investing

**v1, from 2021-01-15 to 2024-01-14.**
1. On the 15th we invest 2 500.00 DUK.
2. It goes into Ania's wrapped account first, as long as the year's limit has room (the account buys GLB and BND half and half).
3. Whatever doesn't fit goes to Tomek's Rzeka account and is split in three equal parts, to the ort, between WEK-EM-A, WEK-IDX-D and BRZ-DIV-D; orts left over go one each to the funds in that order. (In the wrapped account, an odd ort goes to GLB.)

**v2, from 2024-01-15.**
1. On the 15th we invest everything in the joint account above the floor. We count the balance at the end of that day, with everything booked that day except the transfers our house rules make that day. The order of the rows within the day doesn't matter.
2. The floor is 1 000.00 DUK, plus every payment we know will leave the joint account after the 15th and before the next 15th (standing orders and instalments), plus what we spent by card in an average month.
3. The card average is the three full calendar months before the 15th: card payments, less card refunds, less what friends paid us back for shared bills, divided by three and rounded to the ort, half up.
4. The money goes into Ania's wrapped account first, as long as the year's limit has room; the rest goes to Tomek's Rzeka account into BRZ-GLB-A.

## A-HR2 · Which day is the 15th

From 2021-01-15. If the 15th isn't a business day, the rule's day is the next business day. "The next 15th" means the next rule's day.

## A-HR3 · The next car

From 2026-01-15. On a 15th, after that day's investing, if Tomek's Rzeka account is worth 60 000.00 DUK or more and its BRZ-GLB-A is worth 45 000.00 DUK or more, we sell enough BRZ-GLB-A there to bring 45 000.00 DUK — units to 4 places, rounded up, oldest units first — and buy a car with the 45 000.00 DUK; what the sale brings above it stays in the account as cash. Once only.

## A-HR4 · Zosia's savings

From 2026-01-15. On the 15th, 500.00 DUK of what is above the floor (A-HR1 v2 .2) goes from the joint account to Zosia's savings.

## A-HR5 · How we count what we have

From 2021-01-01.
1. What we own together counts half each; if a sum doesn't split into two equal halves, the odd ort is Tomek's.
2. What's only one person's is counted for that person alone.
3. Pay earned but not yet paid counts, at what will reach the account.
4. Workplace-plan contributions count only once the plan has credited them.
5. A tax return's result not yet settled counts as money owed to us or by us, and is shared like the pay it comes from.
