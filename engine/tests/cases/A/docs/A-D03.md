# A-D03 · Nurt Software · the acquisition and your options

- id: A-D03
- issuer: Nurt Software sp.
- issued: 2022-10-03
- received: 2022-10-03

Nurt Software has been acquired by Arka Systems. Your vested options are cashed out: a one-off gross amount of 110 000.00 DUK, paid through payroll with your pay for October 2022, on 10 November 2022. Payroll treats it as pay: contributions, the workplace plan and the advance apply as to any pay. Your employment continues unchanged.
