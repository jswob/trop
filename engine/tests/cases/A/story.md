## The forecast (as we see it on 2026-01-15)

For the years to 2035 we assume:

1. The forecast starts from what the records show on 31 December 2025, at that day's prices; January 2026's investing and Zosia's transfer are left out. From February 2026 we invest a fixed sum on the 15th instead of what A-HR1 v2 gives: 3 000.00 DUK a month in 2026, 100.00 DUK more each January after that. It goes as A-HR1 v2 sends it: the wrapped account first, up to the year's limit, the rest to BRZ-GLB-A in Tomek's Rzeka account.
2. The wrapped limit for 2026 is the published one; for 2027 we take 27 900.00 DUK, and 600.00 DUK more each year after that.
3. Every fund's price moves on each month's last day by the path's rate, and is then rounded to the places its fund quotes in. Paths: **base** +0.40 % a month; **crash** the base, but −50 % on 2027-03-31; **flat** 0; **switch** the base to December 2029, then −0.10 % a month.
4. Distributions are left out. The workplace plans, the savings accounts and the bank accounts stay as they are, outside the forecast. Nothing new goes into Ania's Rzeka account; its units move with the prices like every fund's and are sold with the rest (item 5).
5. On 2035-12-31 we sell everything in the wrapped account and both Rzeka accounts at that day's prices, and pay the tax on it.
6. Money is turned back into January-2026 money by dividing by 1.0025 for each of the 119 months from January 2026 to December 2035.
7. The what-if: from tax year 2030 the gains tax is 25 % instead of 19 %, on every path.
