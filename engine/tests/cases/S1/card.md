## Law

### FAM-1 · Joint and own property
**v1** · in force 2016-01-01 – until replaced · published 2015-09-01 · governs: every marriage, from 2016-01-01
1. Joint: what either spouse earns while married, and what is bought with joint money. Own: what a spouse had before marrying, inherits or is given, and what is bought with their own money.
2. Interest credited to an account belongs to the owners of its money in proportion to their money in it just before the credit; each own-money part is rounded to whole orts, half up, and the rest is joint.
3. Rows of one day are taken in the statement's order.

### FAM-2 · Payments from an account holding joint and own money
**v1** · in force 2016-01-01 – 2025-06-30 · published 2015-09-01 · governs: payments made up to 2025-06-30
1. A payment for common needs (the home, food, bills, holidays together) is taken from joint money; only when none is left, from own money, each spouse's in proportion to it.
2. A payment for one spouse alone is taken from that spouse's own money; only when none is left, from joint money.
3. No claim between the spouses arises from a payment.

**v2** · in force 2025-07-01 – until replaced · published 2025-05-20 · governs: payments made from 2025-07-01
1. A payment for common needs is taken from the joint money and each spouse's own money in proportion to them just before the payment; each own-money part is rounded to whole orts, half up, and the rest is taken from joint money.
2. As v1.
3. As v1.
