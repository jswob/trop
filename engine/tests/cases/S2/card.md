## Law

### MAR-1 · Spouses' property
**v1** · in force 2012-01-01 – until replaced · published 2011-06-30 · governs: every marriage, from 2012-01-01
1. Pay earned while married is joint. What a spouse had before marrying, inherits or is given is their own, and their own taxes, with the interest on them, are their own debts.
2. Common needs take joint money first. A spouse's own debt takes their own money first, then joint money; for joint money so used they owe the other spouse half, due when the marriage ends, without interest.

### GFT-1 · Gift tax
**v1** · in force 2015-01-01 – until replaced · published 2014-09-15 · governs: gifts taken into possession from 2015-01-01
1. The receiver owes gift tax from the day of possession, on the gift's value that day; a car's value is its model and year's value on the latest Motor Register used-car list published by that day.
2. The tax is 5 % of the value from 10 000,00 to 30 000,00 DUK and 9 % of the value above 30 000,00 DUK, rounded to whole dukats, half up.
3. A gift from close family (spouse, children, grandchildren, parents, grandparents, siblings) is tax-free, as if no obligation had arisen, if reported on form SD-3 within 6 months of possession: by the same-numbered day six months on, or that month's last day if it has none.
4. A decision assesses the tax at the amount points 1–2 give, due 14 days after the day it is delivered.
5. Unpaid tax bears interest for each day from the day after the due date to the day of payment, both included, at 10 % a year divided by 365, rounded to whole dukats, half up, when the tax is paid. No interest on interest.
6. A payment counts on the day the account is debited and goes to the tax first, then to interest. No day moves for weekends or holidays.
