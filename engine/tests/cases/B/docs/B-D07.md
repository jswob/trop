# B-D07 · Bank Jodła · mortgage loan agreement 7710-22 (extract)

- id: B-D07
- issuer: Bank Jodła S.A.
- issued: 2022-03-10
- received: 2022-03-10

Borrowers: Piotr Sowa and Aleksandra Wilk, jointly. Purpose: buying the flat at ul. Wrzosowa 12/5, Kłodzin, their home.

1. Amount: 420 000.00 DUK, paid to the seller on the day of the deed. Term: 300 monthly instalments, on the 5th of each month, the first on 5 April 2022. An instalment day that isn't a business day moves to the next business day; the interest is counted to the 5th.
2. Rate: DUKOR 6M plus a margin of 2.10 percentage points, a year. DUKOR 6M is reset on 1 January and 1 July: the fixing of the last business day before the reset day applies until the next reset. The first rate is set from the fixing of the last business day before the payment day.
3. Interest is charged daily on the day's balance at the rate in force that day, over 365 days, from the payment day. The interest of an instalment is what accrued since the last instalment day (for the first, since the payment day), rounded to the ort.
4. Instalments are equal (annuity). The instalment is set again, for the instalments left, at the first instalment after each reset and after each overpayment, so that the loan ends on the same day.
5. Overpayment: allowed on any day. A fee of 2 % of the amount overpaid is charged on overpayments made within 36 months of the payment day; then none. The fee is taken from the amount overpaid; the rest reduces the balance on the day.
6. Payment holidays under the law are granted on notice (MTG-1).
