- received: 2025-06-12

Parties: Halina Sowa, Piotr Sowa, Anna Lis, Marta, Jakub Sowa, the heirs under the certificate of 17 October 2024.

1. Halina Sowa takes: the shares of Piotr Sowa, Anna Lis and Marta in the flat ul. Ogrodowa 2/11; the estate's half of the 325 bonds in register 55-1904-2271; the estate's half of the Bank Olcha account LN73 4400 2011 0000 8817 2264 0001; the refund on Zbigniew's 2024 return (1 844.00 DUK, paid into that account on 21 May 2025). Jakub Sowa keeps his share in the flat. From today the flat belongs to Halina Sowa 13/16 and Jakub Sowa 3/16.
2. The parties value: the flat at 165 000.00 DUK; the bonds at their value today; the account at its balance at the end of 17 May 2024 (46 761.80 DUK).
3. Halina Sowa paid the flat's building fees from 18 May 2024 to today (5 610.00 DUK, 13 payments). Each other heir's share of them is set off against what Halina Sowa pays that heir.
4. Halina Sowa pays each heir the value of what that heir gives up, less the set-off in point 3, rounded down to whole dukats; what is rounded off stays with her. Of each payment to Piotr Sowa, Anna Lis and Marta, 30 937.50 DUK is for the share in the flat.
5. Halina Sowa pays the whole sum into the notary's deposit account today. The notary pays it out on 16 June 2025: to Piotr Sowa to LN45 2090 0017 5521 3380 0002, to Anna Lis to LN61 1140 2004 0000 3402 9921 5503, to Marta to her account, and to Jakub Sowa to LN52 2090 0017 4471 0098 0001.
6. No transfer tax is due: this is a division between heirs, not a sale (TT-1). The notary's fee for this deed, 1 845.00 DUK, was paid by Halina Sowa in cash today; 1 230.00 DUK of it is for the transfer of the shares in the flat.
