# Household D · Halina

Halina lives alone in her own house in Brzezie, near Kłodzin. Her husband Zbigniew died on 17 May 2024. They lived on two pensions from one joint account at Bank Olcha, bought five treasury savings bonds every month, and had a loan on the house, insured on Zbigniew's life. Zbigniew also owned a small flat in Kłodzin, let to a tenant. His estate went to Halina, their three living children and the son of their late son Adam; Halina bought out the children's shares, and her grandson Kuba kept his. The entries run from 1980 to 30 September 2026. Each entry: `date · known <date> · <document or record>`; a fact without a document is stated in our own words.

## The long view (as we see it on 2026-09-30)

D-HR3 runs Halina's money a year at a time from 1 January 2027 until she dies. It assumes:

1. **The start**: on 1 January 2027 the account holds 10 000.00 DUK and the fund 45 000.00 DUK. These are round figures we take as given: the bonds left are redeemed in December 2026 and put into the fund with what the account has above 10 000.00. Ogrodowa stays as it is, 13/16 hers.
2. **Her life**: the chance she dies in the year she turns a (68 in 2027) is q = 0.0120 × 1.105^(a − 68), rounded to 4 decimal places, for a = 68 .. 104; at 105 it is 1. A path draws one uniform number from [0, 1) for each year from 2027 in turn and she dies in the first year whose number is below that year's q. She lives no part of the year she dies in: none of its money is counted.
3. **The index**: every figure that follows prices grows by 3 % a year from 2026 on: a factor 1.03 for each year.
4. **Her pensions**: the survivor pension in full (5 247.88 DUK a month from March 2026), and a part of her own pension (3 048.80 DUK from March 2026): 25 % from January 2027, to the ort, cut so the two together are no more than 3 times `MIN-PENSION` (1 970.00 DUK from March 2026) (SOC-7 v2). On each 1 March from 2027 all three are multiplied by the year before's factor, to the ort. The SIO pays what the law says. Health 9 % of each month's gross, to the ort. The year's PIT is the PIT-3 v3 scale on the year's gross rounded to whole dukats, less 3 900.00, rounded.
5. **Ogrodowa**: the rent 1 976.00 DUK (from March 2026) is multiplied on each 1 March from 2027 by the year before's factor, to the ort; the building fee, 468.00 DUK a month in 2026, each January from 2027 by the year before's factor, to the ort. Halina's year is 13/16 of (the year's rent − the year's fees), to the ort, less PIT-13's 8.5 % of 13/16 of the year's rent, rounded to whole dukats.
6. **Her spending**: 54 000.00 DUK a year in 2026 money, times the factors of 2027 up to the year; and from the year she turns 80 (2039), care at 36 000.00 DUK a year in 2026 money, grown the same way. The year's total to the ort.
7. **The fund**: one return a year, R = 5 % + 16 % × T / √2, where T is Student-t with 4 degrees of freedom, drawn as Z / √(V / 4) with Z a standard normal and V the sum of four squared standard normals (drawn after Z); never below −95 %; R rounded to 6 decimal places. The fund's value at the year's end is its value at the start times (1 + R), to the ort. Its gains are not taxed.
8. **The year's end**: the fund's return first; then the year's money (pensions less health and PIT, plus Ogrodowa, less spending) goes onto the account. What is above 15 000.00 DUK goes into the fund; if the account is below 0, fund units are sold to bring it back to 0. If the fund can't, the account stays below 0: the money has run dry, in the first year that happens. Her net worth at a year's end is the account plus the fund.
9. **The law** stays as it is in 2026. Nothing else comes in or goes out.
10. **Zero volatility**: every R is 5 %.

We draw paths with Python's `random.Random`, seed 20261001 + the path's number: first her life's numbers, then the returns of the 38 years 2027 .. 2064 in order (drawn whether or not she lives them), each with `gauss(0, 1)` five times.
