## Law

### SEP-1 · Separate property
**v1** · in force 2000-01-01 – until replaced · published 1999-06-01 · governs: marriages under a contract of separate property
1. Each spouse alone owns what the contract gives them. A rise or fall in a thing's value is its owner's; what a sale brings in is the seller's.

### CGT-1 · Units, sales and tax
**v1** · in force 2019-01-01 – until replaced · published 2018-07-02 · governs: units held, and sales made, from 2019-01-01
1. Units are held in batches: each purchase is a batch, acquired on its day, costing the price paid plus the purchase fee. Units received as a gift form a batch with the giver's acquisition day and the giver's cost for them; a gift is not a sale, and no tax is owed on it.
2. Units taken from a batch cost the batch's remaining cost times the units taken divided by its remaining units, rounded to whole orts, half up; the units left keep the rest of the cost.
3. A sale takes units from the seller's batches oldest first, by acquisition day. Its fee is spread over the batches it takes in proportion to their units: each share but the youngest batch's is rounded to whole orts, half up, and the youngest batch takes the rest. The gain on each batch's part is its units times the price, less its fee share, less its cost.
4. A part held more than 3 years on the day of sale is left out, gain or loss: the 3 years end on the same-numbered day three years after acquisition, or on that month's last day if it has no such day, and the sale must come after that day.
5. A year's taxed gain is the sum of the gains and losses not left out, of that year's sales, rounded to whole dukats, half up; a negative sum counts as 0 and is not carried over. The tax is 19 % of the taxed gain, rounded to whole dukats, half up, due by 30 April of the next year.

**v1** · from 2008-05-10
