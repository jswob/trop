# SURF-01 · No bare figures

**A passage to write; it has no key.** Session 3 writes it; session 5's reading test reads it.

**Story.** House rule A-1: "On the 15th of each month, keep 1 000.00 DUK in the current account and move the rest to savings."

**Write:** the rule, so that every figure in it is a named fact: the floor 1 000.00 is a fact of the house rules (A-1, with its start date); the 15th may stay bare, as a day of the month. The model must flag a named figure nothing reads.

**What the passage shows:** which figures are facts and which may stay bare (days of the month, 12, 100); no amount appears inside a rule's arithmetic as a literal.
