# SURF-02 · Surface words

**A passage to write; it has no key.**

**Story.** A rule from an estate's division: "If the flat's value at the division is more than its value at the death, each heir's buyout is the share of the division's value; summed over the heirs, the buyouts are what the keeper pays. For an heir with no share, the buyout does not apply (it is not 0). A heir who received an advance has it subtracted, which can leave a negative buyout: he pays in."

**Write:** the rule, so that a reader sees: the condition comparing two amounts ("is more than", a question, not a statement "is"); the sum over heirs as one aggregate; "does not apply" kept apart from a negative amount; the language's one order of words.

**What the passage shows:** "is" vs "equals?"; minus vs not applicable; a condition vs its value; an aggregate over heirs.
