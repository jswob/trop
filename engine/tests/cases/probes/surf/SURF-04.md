# SURF-04 · Stated once, checked before it runs

**A passage to write; it has no key.**

**Story.** A contribution formula: "9.76 % of gross, on gross only until the year's gross to date reaches the cap; rounded half up to 0.01". It is used three times: in the monthly payroll, in the yearly return's check of what was withheld, and in a what-if with a raise.

**Write:** the formula once, used in the three places; ids in ASCII; then try to define the same id a second time and show that it is refused before anything runs.

**What the passage shows:** one statement, its consequences derived; a formula written once for a long law formula; a redefined id refused; everything checked before it runs.
