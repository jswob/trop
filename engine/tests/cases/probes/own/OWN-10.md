# OWN-10 · Parts that arrive: labelled by a law written once

**Story.** Ania and Tomek, married under community property, keep savings accounts whose balance is kept as **parts**: each amount carries whose it is (and, at a fund account, which purchase brought it in). One rule, written once, labels every amount that arrives by the kind of event that brought it (tally 2.1, LANGUAGE §3.6). Each part below is its own small case, in January to June 2026; the parties are things of the prelude kind `parties` (Ania and Tomek persons, `ania_tomek` their couple, others as named). Amounts are DUK; a savings account's step is 0.01 DUK, a fund account's 1 unit.

**The rules every part shares** (unless the part says otherwise):
- R1. An inheritance arriving at an owned account is the heir's; any other arrival is the couple's.
- R2. Money moved from an owned account to another keeps its labels: each party's share is what the move took of that party's parts over what it took.
- R3. An outflow takes the couple's part first.
- R4. Every split of an arrival rounds to the place's step by the largest remainder; parts are whole steps and sum to the balance.

Question: each party's part, and the total, at the end of 31 January.
