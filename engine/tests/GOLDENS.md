## How a golden is restated

- **The prototype's fixtures** are `engine/spec/verification/blocks/cases/01..15.json` and `pins-1-7.json`. A restated text writes their figures as stated rows, every figure a row whose note names its block (Tally explicit).
- **The vector** is refcore's run of the text (`m1/vectors.tsv`, groups `goldens-…`, held by the runner's `m1_goldens`). No expected value is worked out by hand: the golden is the prototype's figure, the vector refcore's bytes, and this page records whether they agree.
- **A difference** is allowed only where `goldens.md` names the finding that moves it; any other is a STOP-REPORT in `GAPS.md`, and a wrong number from refcore on a valid household text is the M1 brief's §3 patch case.
- **The law-level figures reuse household X's law** (`integration/amendments/real11/c1`): X is the prototype's household rewritten, and its employment law is the prototype's `employment_pl@2022` (X's wife at 7 000 gross prints the pin `wife_tax/net[2026-10]`, 5 101.67, byte for byte). `m1/goldens/law/golden.tally` adds the prototype's `my_job` (12 000 gross; 12 480 from 2027 by `index.wage`).

## The prototype's bank history (M2, #114)

M2 tracks it: `m2/bank/ledger.json` (synthetic, seed 20261017), its record parts and R7, the prototype's reality (load.ts REALITY: blocks 01, 03, 04, 05, 07), restated over it (`m2/bank/README.md`): the bank half `bank.tally` and the forward half `r7/` (the daily spending, the 15th rule with IKE first and OKI, the ETF units, the side business, the yearly returns, net worth, FIRE), a header per horizon. G1–G5 now hold below, and 13 of the 20 pins are stored (C4, 2026-11-15; vectors `goldens/bank_*`, `goldens/r7_*` in `m2/vectors.tsv`, group `goldens-records`; the engine prints refcore's bytes on every new one already, so their `for` is `-`). R7's ETF price is the prototype's monthly recurrence, `before * pow(1.06, 1 / 12)` with the factor stated as a row, not the closed form's transcendental `pow … to`, which the engine builds at M6.

- **L-26 and its twin.** `r7/returns.tally` files each spouse's PIT-37 alone (L-26 bars the joint return beside the ryczałt, as M1's P7 reads it); `r7/joint.tally` is its twin with the prototype's joint return. On the twin every stored figure is the prototype's to the grosz (net worth on 2027-06-30 and 2027-12-31, OKI's tax of 2027 and 2030, `pit_joint/due` −259 and −1 245), so where the two differ, L-26 alone moves the figure: "moved by L-26" below is a machine check, not a reading.
- **P2, the long horizons.** refcore builds each projection's walk from its day to the run's end and keeps it, so the 15th rule's monthly projection costs memory that grows with the horizon: to 2028-06-30, 0.84 GB, 9 s (one ask; 40 s for all of `to2028`'s); to 2031-01-31, 3.6 GB, 22 s; to 2030-12-31 without the rule, 0.26 GB, 3 s. The horizons 2036 (`r7/to2036.tally`), 2050 (`to2050.tally`, G6 with `raise.tally`, OKI 2040) and 2056 (`to2056.tally`, G10 with `infl5.tally`) pass the 8 GB cap on refcore and on refcore_fast alike (memcap stopped them, refcore then refcore_fast: at 8.2 and 8.3 GB after 46 and 43 s; at 8.3 GB after 50 and 55 s; at 8.4 and 8.7 GB after 55 and 57 s). Their texts load and are tracked. **P45** (the coordinator, by the user's standing rules: a run past 8 GB is an engine benchmark, a full-scale-only figure unjudged by the interpreter): the five figures with a prototype value become pin checks against the prototype's own figures, on the joint twin, `m2/bank/pins_long.tsv` and its checker `pins_long.py` (`--engine PATH`; `--check` in `run.py --self-test`); the two digests stay unstored (below). The engine (quick profile, 2026-11-15) runs all three horizons: `to2036` 4 s, 2.1 GB; `to2050` 80 s, 12.1 GB; `to2056` 103 s, 12.5 GB, so the checker is no target of `check` (8 GB a run). **The gate's timing run takes these three horizons** as benchmarks, each with its header, `bank.tally`, `r7/forward.tally`, `r7/business.tally`, `r7/joint.tally` and the 34 record parts: `r7/to2036.tally` (`--ask pin_2036`), `r7/to2050.tally` (`--ask pin_oki_tax_2040`, and G6's `g6_net_worth`, `g6_fire`), `r7/to2056.tally` (`--ask pin_2056`, and G10's `g10_net_worth`), for `engine/perf/M2.md`'s list.
