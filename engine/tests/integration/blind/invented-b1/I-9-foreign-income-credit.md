# I-9 · Income in talars: a credit for foreign tax, foreign cash as lots, and a refund that may come late

**Story.** Marek lives in Lenia. He holds shares in Orvel, a company of the Duchy of Varnia, and an account in talars (`TAL`, 2 decimal places) at a Varnian bank. On 1 January 2027 the account holds 1 000.00 TAL, one lot with a dukat cost of 4 050.00.

| Day (2027) | Event |
|---|---|
| Fri 12 Mar | Orvel dividend, 2 000.00 TAL gross; Varnia withholds 25 %; the net is credited to the account |
| Mon 28 Jun | Interest on the account, 120.00 TAL; Varnia withholds nothing |
| Thu 30 Sep | Orvel dividend, 3 000.00 TAL gross; Varnia withholds 25 %; net credited |
| Mon 13 Dec | Marek converts 3 000.00 TAL from the account to dukats at the bank's rate of 4.2300 DUK per TAL |

Central bank rates, DUK per 1 TAL: Thu 11 Mar 2027 4.2150; Fri 25 Jun 2027 4.1020; Wed 29 Sep 2027 4.3075. No day in this card is a holiday.

On 15 January 2028 Marek claims from Varnia the refund its treaty with Lenia allows: 10 % of each dividend's gross. Varnia's office may pay it, or refuse it; when it pays is not known. Marek files his 2027 return on 30 April 2028.

**Rules.**
1. (Income Act) A resident is taxed on dividends and interest from anywhere at 19 % of the gross amount. The year's tax, less the credits of rule 3, is rounded to whole dukats, half up, and is due by a return on 30 April of the next year.
2. An amount received in a foreign currency is converted at the central bank's rate of the last business day before the day it is received, rounded half up to whole minor units, amount by amount. Foreign tax withheld from an amount, and any part of it refunded, is converted at the same rate.
3. (Credit Act) Foreign tax paid on an item of income is credited against the Lenian tax on that item, up to that Lenian tax. Foreign tax paid is the tax withheld less any part refunded before the return is filed.
4. If foreign tax is refunded after the return is filed, the item's credit is recomputed as if the refund had come before; the difference, rounded to whole dukats, half up, is added to the tax of the year the refund is received, due 30 April of the next year.
5. (Gains Act) Foreign cash is held in lots. Cash received as income is a lot whose cost is that cash converted under rule 2. A conversion of foreign cash to dukats is a disposal, matched to lots oldest first; the gain is the dukats received minus the cost of the cash matched. A year's net gain is taxed at 19 %, rounded to whole dukats, half up, due 30 April of the next year.

**Question.** For 2027: each item's dukat amount, Lenian tax and credit; the lots used by the conversion and its gain; and the 2027 tax due on 30 April 2028. Then any amount added to the 2028 tax, with its due date. Answer for three outcomes: the refund is received on 20 March 2028; it is received on 17 July 2028; it is refused.

**What makes it hard.** Each amount lives in a second currency, with its own rate picked by a business-day rule, and the dukat value it gets becomes the cost of cash that later meets a second regime when it is converted. Withheld tax, the item it was withheld from, and the credit are tied together item by item. The credit depends on whether a later event happened before a filing date, so one unknown date gives three outcomes, and in one of them the effect lands in another year's tax. Lots of foreign cash carry from before the year.
