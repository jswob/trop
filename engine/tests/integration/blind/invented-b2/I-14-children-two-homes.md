# I-14 · Two children between two homes: one fact read by three laws on three clocks

**Story.** Wiktor and Zofia separated in 2025 and keep separate households. Their children are Tymon, born 22 June 2017, and Ula, born 3 November 2021. The share of nights each child spends at Wiktor's, the rest being at Zofia's:

| Child | 1 Jan – 30 Apr 2027 | 1 May – 31 Aug 2027 | From 1 Sep 2027 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tymon | 15 % | 50 % | 70 % |
| Ula | 15 % | 15 % | 15 % |

A share applies evenly to every night of its span; a month has as many nights as days.

Zofia has been paid both children's benefit since 2025. Wiktor claims Tymon's benefit on 10 November 2027. Monthly net incomes: Wiktor 9 000.00, Zofia 6 000.00, unchanged throughout. Maintenance for each child was last assessed by the court in December 2025, on Wiktor having 15 % of each child's nights and on the same incomes. On 20 September 2027 Wiktor applies for Tymon's maintenance to be reassessed; no other application is made.

**Rules.**
1. (Child Benefit Act) For each child under 18 a benefit of 300.00 a month is paid on the 15th, for that month, to the child's main carer.
2. The main carer for a calendar quarter is the parent with whom the child spent more nights in the previous quarter. On a tie, the previous quarter's main carer stays.
3. A new main carer is paid from the later of the first month of the quarter for which they become main carer and the month after their claim. Until then the previous main carer is paid. A month already paid is never paid again.
4. For a child under 6 on the first day of a month, a care allowance of 120.00 for that month is paid with the benefit, to whoever receives the benefit.
5. (Maintenance Act) For each child, the parent with the smaller share of nights pays the other a monthly maintenance of 18 % of the payer's monthly net income times (1 − 2 × the payer's share of nights), rounded half up to whole minor units. When the shares are equal, nothing is due.
6. An assessment stands until the court reassesses it on a parent's application. The new assessment is made on the shares and incomes on the day of the application and applies from the first month after it.
7. Maintenance for a month is due on its 1st. When the parents owe each other for the same month, only the difference is paid, by the parent who owes more.
8. (Income Act) A parent may claim a child relief of 95.00 for each month of the year in which the child spent at least 40 % of its nights with that parent. When both parents qualify for a month, each takes half.

**Question.** For each month from January 2027 to March 2028: who is paid each child's benefit and care allowance, and how much; the maintenance owed for each child, by whom; the single payment made on the 1st, its payer and its amount. Then each parent's child relief for 2027.

**What makes it hard.** One fact, the share of nights, is read by three acts in three ways: the benefit by a count over the previous quarter, with a tie rule and a claim that delays the change; maintenance through an assessment that stays frozen when the fact changes until someone applies, so a change in May has no effect at all; the relief month by month against a threshold, shared when both parents pass it. The direction of a payment can flip for one child while the other's stays, and the two net into one transfer between two households. An age threshold read on the first day of each month ends an allowance part way through the year. The rules name no parent and no child.
