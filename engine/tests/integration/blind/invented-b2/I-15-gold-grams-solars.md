# I-15 · Gold in grams, priced in solars: a fee taken in metal, coins that bear the levy, two price paths

**Story.** Adam holds a metal account at Vault Mira in the Free City of Orsk. Its gold is fine gold, counted to 0.001 g and priced per gram in solars (`SOL`, 2 decimal places). On 1 January 2027 the account holds 60.000 g, with a cost of 14 400.00 DUK, and no solar cash.

He buys for 3 000.00 DUK on 5 January, 5 April, 5 July and 5 October 2027. On 10 October 2027 he has 30.000 g made into coins of 916 parts per thousand, containing 30.000 g of fine gold, and delivered to him; the minting fee is 40.00 DUK. He makes no purchase after 2027. On 3 March 2028 he sells the coins to a dealer in Lenia for 7 300.00 DUK. On 1 July 2028 he sells 40.000 g from the account.

| Day (2027) | 5 Jan | 31 Mar | 5 Apr | 30 Jun | 5 Jul | 30 Sep | 5 Oct | 10 Oct | 31 Dec |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SOL per g | 130.00 | 129.00 | 128.50 | 133.00 | 134.20 | 137.40 | 138.00 | 139.10 | 141.00 |
| DUK per SOL | 1.9200 | 1.9300 | 1.9350 | 1.9500 | 1.9600 | 1.9750 | 1.9800 | 1.9820 | 1.9900 |

Prices in 2028 are unknown. Two paths are asked, each flat for the whole year: low, 120.00 SOL per g and 1.9000 DUK per SOL; high, 150.00 and 2.0500.

**Rules.** (Vault Mira's terms; the Gains Act and the Value-Added Levy Act of Lenia)
1. (Vault) A purchase takes a fee of 1.5 % of the dukats paid, rounded half up to whole minor units. The rest is converted to solars at the day's rate, rounded down to whole minor units, and added to the solar cash; the vault credits the most whole thousandths of a gram the cash buys and keeps the remainder as solar cash.
2. (Vault) A sale from the account pays grams × price × rate in dukats, rounded half up to whole minor units, with no fee.
3. (Vault) On the last day of each calendar quarter, after everything else that day, the vault takes 0.12 % of the grams held as its storage fee, rounded up to 0.001 g.
4. (Gains Act) The gold in one account is one pool. A purchase adds its grams and its whole dukat cost, fee included. Gold leaving the pool takes the pool's average cost per gram times its grams, rounded half up to whole minor units.
5. (Gains Act) Gold taken as a fee is a disposal at its value that day, worked as in rule 2. That value is also a cost deducted from the year's net gain.
6. (Gains Act) Delivery of gold to its holder is not a disposal. The delivered gold is a holding of its own, whose cost is its pool cost plus any minting fee and levy paid on it.
7. (Gains Act) A gain is proceeds minus cost. A year's net gain, after deducted costs, is taxed at 19 %, rounded to whole dukats, half up, due 30 April of the next year. A net loss is carried to the next year only.
8. (Gains Act) Solar cash in a metal account, up to 50.00 SOL, is outside the Act.
9. (Levy Act) A supply of investment gold, meaning fine gold of at least 995 parts per thousand in bars or in an account, is exempt. Making account gold into coins of lower fineness and delivering them is a supply of the coins by the vault, at the gold's value that day, worked as in rule 2, plus the minting fee; it bears 23 %, rounded half up to whole minor units, paid by the holder on delivery.
10. (Levy Act) A sale by a person not registered under the Act bears no levy.

**Question.** The grams, pool cost and solar cash after each event of 2027; each fee's grams, value and gain; the levy on the coins and their cost; the 2027 tax. Then, on each 2028 path: the coins' gain; the 1 July sale's proceeds, cost and gain; each 2028 fee; and the 2028 tax with its due date.

**What makes it hard.** Holdings are a quantity in grams with its own rounding, distinct from money, priced in a second currency and converted on the day. A fee paid in metal is at once a disposal and a deductible cost under one act. An average-cost pool is state carried across years, and gold leaving it in kind becomes a new holding that takes its cost with it. The levy reaches one change of form but not the bars or a private sale, by the kind of the goods and the seller. A small cash remainder carries between purchases. The future runs on two named price paths.
