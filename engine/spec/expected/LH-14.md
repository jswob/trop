Model: `tmp/design/models/LH-14.tally`. Case file: `tmp/verification/cases/LH-14.md`. "PA n" is item n of the case's `## Published answer`. A quoted name is the matching `published_answers` entry in the front matter. "src n" is the case's source number.

The model has four axes:
- `chart_cut`: down, half-even, half-up, up.
- `ex_round`: footnote, statute.
- `guarantee`: applied, ignored.
- `illus_cut`: dime, dollar.

An ask fans out only over the axes it reaches.

## Derivations
