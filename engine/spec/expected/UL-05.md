# UL-05 expected

Model: `tmp/design/models/UL-05.tally`. The model has two axes:
- `ul05_school` is the rounding of the school-start supplement ÷ 12. `per_item` is the ministry's practice: 8,33 each, then summed.
- `ul05_avg` is how the łączna kwota is averaged. `split` is the ministry's practice: one figure per run of equal months.

The ministry's figures are `per_item` with `split`. The figure noted against each row is that reading unless the row says otherwise. Every amount here is from January 2016 or later, so the `payable` rule is `base v dz2015_995`.

Known dates: the 100,00 zł school supplement from 2006-12-05 (Dz.U. 2006 nr 222 poz. 1630, governing from 1 September 2006), the taper and the 20,00 zł minimum from 2015-07-17 (Dz.U. 2015 poz. 995), the amounts and the criterion from 2015-08-27 (Dz.U. 2015 poz. 1238), and the examples' stipulations from the model's `on`, 2026-10-18. Every ask runs at `on`, so it sees them all.
