# MP-08 expected: DSUE portability, including a second deceased spouse

Case file: `tmp/verification/cases/MP-08.md`. Model: `tmp/design/models/MP-08.tally`. Locators name the sub-case under "Published answer" and the `published_answers` entry.

The model has no axis, so each ask prints one line. Money prints in dollars; `gift_credit` is a `num USD`.

**Law dates.** Round 3 uses `at` where a figure depends on the law at a gift. Where it would change nothing, the def runs at the ask's cut, 2026-10-19:
- **C1's credit** reads the § 2001(c) table at the gift's date. The gift is dated 2002, so `gift_tentative` and `exclusion_tentative` run at 2002-01-01 (§4.3). They read the schedule of Pub. L. 94-455 (`tra1976`, known 1976-10-04, governing 1977 to 2009), not Pub. L. 112-240's (`atra2012`, from 2013), which no ask reads now. The bracket over $750,000 is the same in both, so the credit is unchanged.
- **B** asks for W's applicable exclusion amount for gifts in the second half of 2015. `aea_gifts` is keyed by year, which would give it the law of 2015-01-01 (§4.3, rule 2). The final regulation governs only from 2015-06-12, so its rule would have no candidate (`RUN-NO-RULE`); round 2 missed this. The def now runs at `gifts_from[w, 2015]`, 2015-07-01, the first day of the second half. The figures are as before.
- **The exclusion** is keyed by year and read at its year, so an `at` on `bea_at_death`, `aea_left` or `gift_excess` would change nothing.
- **The regulation's rules** (`cfr_dsue_method`, `cfr_portability_method`, governing from 2015-06-12) run at the cut. The examples apply the final regulations to deaths and gifts before that day (H1 and E's H in 2011, the 2002 and 2012 gifts, the gift to Y in January 2015), and the temporary regulations aren't in the case. An `at` on those deaths or gifts would leave the rules with no candidate. The deaths of 2015 dated by year (W in A; H in C1, C2 and D1) would read at 2015-01-01, also before the final regulations govern (see `gaps/D6R3-MP1.md`).

**Known dates.** The Rev. Procs are now known from their release with the IRS's news releases (§4.5), 2011-10-20 (IR-2011-104) and 2014-10-30 (IR-2014-104), instead of the Bulletins' dates. T.D. 9884 only changes a cross-reference after a renumbering, so the rules stay with T.D. 9725 (2015-06-16). The other versions keep their dates: Pub. L. 107-16 (2001-06-07), Pub. L. 111-312 (2010-12-17), Pub. L. 112-240 (2013-01-02) and the examples' stipulations (2026-10-19). Every version a figure reads governs what it is read for and is known by the cut.

These changes leave the figures as they were:
- The DSUE computation reads the deceased spouse's own `aea`, a read at a computed key, which is the basic exclusion amount alone for every deceased spouse here (none had a prior marriage).
- A gift's DSUE is net of what the donor's earlier gifts applied of the same spouse's DSUE. Only E has two gifts from one donor, and the earlier one, to X, applied none. The gift itself now drops out by its date compared with itself (§4.4), no longer by key.
- `last_spouse` and `gift_spouse` now take `argmax` over the deaths, which orders these year-dated deaths (§5.10): H2 (2014, or 2015-06-30 in B) after H1 (2011).
