# CH-04 expected: US real GDP 2019:Q4 by vintage

Model: `models/CH-04.tally`. Case: `tmp/verification/cases/CH-04.md`.

Each Philadelphia Fed vintage is a version known on the last day of its month. The case dates a vintage only by its month ("the middle of the month", no day), and LANGUAGE §4.5 gives such a source the month's last day. So an ask `as known 2020-02-29` sees vintage ROUTPUT20M2 and nothing later. ROUTPUT26M10 is the exception: its month ends after the model's `on`, so it is known 2026-09-30, the workbook's internal timestamp. BEA releases are versions known on their release days. Levels print as exact decimals: "19219.8000" is 19219.8 and "19222.0000" is 19222.

Round 2: the vintage day moved from the 15th to the month's last day (§4.5), and every vintage ask and `as if {known …}` pin moved with it. No figure changes: each vintage ask is still cut on its vintage's own known day, and no other version of `routput` falls between the old and the new cut.

Readings:
- the vintage day is the month's last day (26M10: the workbook's timestamp). Each vintage ask is cut on that same day, so the choice changes no figure;
- `disp = half-up` for the four-decimal growth rates: `down` gives 2.0807 for the first release, against 2.0808 printed. For BEA's headline to 0.1, `half-up` gives 2.1 for all three releases; `down` gives 2.0 for the advance and second;
- growth X_t and X_{t−1} are read from the same vintage (the case: the documentation does not say so in words).

## Arithmetic

GX = 100 [(X_t / X_{t−1})^4 − 1] in exact fractions, then rounded with Decimal:

`python3 -c "from fractions import Fraction as F; from decimal import Decimal as D, ROUND_HALF_UP as H, ROUND_DOWN as W; g=lambda a,b: 100*((F(a)/F(b))**4-1); dd=lambda x: D(x.numerator)/D(x.denominator); [print(n, dd(g(a,b)), 'half-up', dd(g(a,b)).quantize(D('0.0001'),rounding=H), 'down', dd(g(a,b)).quantize(D('0.0001'),rounding=W), 'to 0.1', dd(g(a,b)).quantize(D('0.1'),rounding=H), dd(g(a,b)).quantize(D('0.1'),rounding=W), 'change', D(a)-D(b)) for n,a,b in [('first','19219.8','19121.1'),('second','19220.5','19121.1'),('third','19222.0','19121.1'),('recent','20985.4','20843.3')]]"`
