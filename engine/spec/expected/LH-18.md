## How the figures are read

- **Model.** Makeham one-year survival with A = 0.00022, B = 0.0000027 and c = 1.124, taken as exact decimals. l runs from 100,000 at age 20 by a running product. Interest is i = 0.05 and the table closes at age 130. The formulas are the workbook's (case: Law references).
- **Printed figures.** The 36 single-figure asks (`lx_*`, `qx_*`, `adue_*`, `ins_*`, `ins2_*`, `adue10_*`, `endw10_*`, `adue20_*`, `endw20_*`, `pe5_*`, `pe10_*`, `pe20_*`) round the model's value to the printed digits, by the axis `print_round`.
- **The rounding reading is the nearest** (`half-up` or `half-even`). The two print the same figure here, since no value sits on a tie. `down` (truncation) is ruled out: it gives 99,338.2 for l_40, 0.005914 for q_65 and 14 other misses (derivation 1). The case states no mode (case: Missing facts, third bullet).
- **Unrounded figures.** `stored_40`, `stored_65` and `stored_80` print the twelve values with no rounding. They should agree with the workbook's stored values to about 15 significant digits, not to every digit: the workbook computes in binary floating point, while the model uses the exact decimals and correctly rounded `exp` and `ln` (`surv` to 1e-15). The case's check of q_40 from the exact decimals, 0.0005272204427949764…, agrees with the stored 5.2722044279496227E-4 to 16 digits.
- **Closing age.** The closing age of 130 is the workbook's; the PDF doesn't state one (case: Missing facts, second bullet). The index `ages` now cites it (`v workbook2022`). Each annuity is the workbook's backward recursion: `step` reads age x + 1, and `last` gives 1 at age 130, the first payment alone, as the workbook's empty cell D120 does.

**Round 2.** No figure changes. `lives` is now `rule init excepts step when x = 20` with `rule step` reading x − 1, and `adue` and `adue2` are recursions read back from age 130 instead of sums (§5.10 allows reads at later keys). The recursion and the round-1 sum give exactly the same numbers: ä_x = 1 + v·p_x·ä_(x+1) is that sum written backward. Both versions are now known 2022-10-31, the last day of the 2022-10 edition (§4.5); no ask has a cut, so the asks read at the model's `on` and see both.
