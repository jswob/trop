# UL-01 expected

Model: `tmp/design/models/UL-01.tally`. Every published figure below is to the grosz. No source states the rounding, so the model carries two axes: `ul01_mode` (down, half-even, half-up), applied to both the base and the contribution, and `ul01_base9` (whether 9% is taken of the rounded base or the unrounded one). The figures match the readings named in each row. Where a reading gives a different figure, that is stated.

Known dates. The law rows are known from their promulgation: the 9% rate, the revenue band and the 60/100/180% shares from 2021-11-23 (Dz.U. 2021 poz. 2105, governing from 2022); the minimum wages from 2024-09-13 (2025) and 2025-09-15 (2026); the GUS average wages from 2025-01-24 (M.P. 2025 poz. 84) and 2026-01-26 (M.P. 2026 poz. 117). The ZUS page's shares (nobiz, collab, karta 2026) are known from 2026-02-02 and the brochure's Tabela 4 column shares from 2026-02-28. Every ask runs at the model's `on`, 2026-10-18, so it sees them all. The brochure's Tabela 4 still prints the 2025 wage and minimum wage named below.
