# CB-10 expected

Model: `tmp/design/models/CB-10.tally`. Case: `tmp/verification/cases/CB-10.md`.

The model has five axes, each for a rounding the decision or the note doesn't state:
- `tp_cut`: the taxpayers' £1 lines.
- `gain_cut`: HMRC's gain.
- `pen_cut`: the penalties.
- `pct_cut`: the taxpayers' 66.62%.
- `note_cut`: the case note's rounded figures.

The law defs run at law date 2016-04-06, the start of tax year 2016-17. The versions are known as follows: `tcga_1992` 1992-03-06 (Royal Assent), `fa2016_s83` 2016-09-15 (Royal Assent; governs gains from 2016-04-06), `facts2022` 2022-02-01 (the decision's release date). All are known before the cut K = 2026-10-18.

The two tax charges of para 2 are givens of the def `tax_charged` (§5.10 step 1), citing the decision. The ask `tax_charge` reads them back; `penalties`, `effective_tax_rate` and `case_note_effective_rate` are computed from them. The first closure figure of para 8(9) is computed by its stated method, and is a hole until two rows arrive.

The taxpayers' printed computation doesn't add up as printed (case, Transcription notes, second bullet). The model computes the lines from the francs and rates, so its taxpayers' totals differ from the printed ones by a few pounds. Each such row says by how much and why.
