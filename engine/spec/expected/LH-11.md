# LH-11 expected: pointers for `models/LH-11.tally`

Case file: `tmp/verification/cases/LH-11.md`. "PA" means the front matter's `published_answers` entry with that `name`. All of them are under `## Published answer` too.

The model has two axes:
- `lh11_mode` is the unstated rounding mode, `down` or `half-up`. It is used for the figures as printed and for any carried rounding.
- `lh11_step` decides whether a rounded intermediate is reused. Under `exact`, every intermediate is carried exact. Under `printed`, each intermediate that a solution prints rounded and then reuses is carried at its printed places:
  - Q60: the payment, to 0.01;
  - Q87: i, to 0.0001;
  - Q89: the payment, to 0.01, and the annual rate, to 0.0001;
  - Q130: the three factors, to 0.0001;
  - Q133: the payment, to 0.01, a_192, to 0.0001, and 1 − v^n, to 0.00001.

Answers are matched to the nearest choice. The `*_choice` asks print a key of `choice`: `opt_a` … `opt_e` stand for (A) … (E).

Versions, and the rule of LANGUAGE §4.5 that dates each one:
- `v_soa_s` holds the solutions' method. It is dated only by a month (the update history ends "October 2022"), so it is known on the month's last day, 2022-10-31.
- `v_soa_q` holds the questions' data. They are stipulations, known from the model's `on` (2026-10-18).

Every ask is read at the model's `on`, and no figure changes from round 1.
