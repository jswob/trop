# RC-01 expected values

Model: `models/RC-01.tally`. Case: `tmp/verification/cases/RC-01.md`.

Locators name the case file's heading `## Published answer`, the bold example heading under it, and the item number. The matching entry of the front matter's `published_answers` is given by its `name`.

Every Derek and Rodney figure depends on no axis. Every Tiana figure is read under `uc_taper = ex063` and `uc_de_minimis = dm300`, which are the parameters the example states (0.63 and £300). The other options print as extra lines and are not checked against the case.

Every ask runs at the model's `on` date, 2026-10-18, as law date and cut. The rules cite the enacting instruments (`ucr_2013`, `ucr_2015_345`, `ucr_2018_65`, `ucr_2021_1283`), all of which govern that date. `ucr_2015_345` governs from 6 April 2016 as made. Under this cut its span is 2018-04-11.., the `moves` of `ucr_2018_65` (known 2018-01-22), which is the latest of the three postponements. The reg 54A rules, the £300, Step 3 of reg 57(2) and the loss rule of reg 57A(1)(a) cite `ucr_2018_65`, which gave that text its present wording. Under `dm2500`, `dm_temporary` is read from the row of `ucr_2018_65_dm`. That version's span, 2018-04-11..2019-03-31, is moved by `det_2026` (known 2026-01-20), the latest of the eight determinations, to 2018-04-11..2027-03-31. That span contains the law date. The values below are the same as in rounds 1 and 2: no figure the asks print moved.
