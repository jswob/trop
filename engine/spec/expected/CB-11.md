Model: `tmp/design/models/CB-11.tally`. Case: `tmp/verification/cases/CB-11.md`.

There are two axes:
- `art26_count`, the art. 26 ust. 1 reading. `after_20` counts the full years beyond 20, and `from_20` also counts the 20th year. Every Example 1 and 2 figure the page prints fits `after_20` only.
- `krus_ratio`. `exact_ratio` applies the fraction of months; `ratio_2dp` rounds it to two decimals first. The page's Examples 1 and 2 fit `ratio_2dp`, and the other KRUS page (ex3) fits `exact_ratio`.

Amounts are `num PLN` and print exact.

The versions are known as follows: `usr_1990` 1991-01-26 (the act's promulgation, Dz.U. 1991 nr 7 poz. 24), `reg883_2004` 2004-04-30 (OJ L 166), and the two KRUS pages' versions 2026-10-18 (the examples' stipulations, from `on`; the pages carry no date). No version governs a closed span, and the defs have no time key, so they run at the cut K = 2026-10-18. `full_years` and `suppl_steps` are `int`. The figures are those of round 1.
