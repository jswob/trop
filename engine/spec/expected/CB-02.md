# CB-02 expected

Model: `tmp/design/models/CB-02.tally`. Case file: `tmp/verification/cases/CB-02.md`. "Published answer" means that section of the case file, with steps a) to d) of Przykład 5 (MF Objaśnienia, pp. 9-10). Every Ewa ask is at keys `ewa, 2021`, so the law date is 2021-01-01. At that date every law version governs: `pit_2019` (the scale and the kwota zmniejszająca, from 2020-01-01), `amend_2020` (the cap and ust. 5, from 2021-01-01), `abol_2008` (art. 27g ust. 2 first sentence) and `op_2005` (the rounding). All are known before the cut, so no figure changed in round 2.

The axis `rate_reading` reaches only the asks that read the exemption-with-progression rate. Its options are `down` and `half-up` (the rate shortened to 0.0001 before it is applied, as printed) and `exact` (applied unrounded). The source prints only the shortened reading. Its two modes can't be told apart here: 16.3436 % gives 16.34 % either way.

Every Ewa result also reaches the assumption `health[ewa, 2021] = 0`. The example shows no health contribution (case file, "Missing facts and ambiguities"). The tax-due asks also reach `work_area_of[ewa, 2021] = land`, an assumption that the cap applies.
