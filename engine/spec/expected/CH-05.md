Readings, which the asks below name:
- Table 1's Total expenses row needs no rounding: it is the sum over single ages 0 to 17 of each age's group average (Table 1 note), so 3 × each group, and the printed rounded values reproduce it exactly.
- `disp = half-up` for every printed rounding. `down` gives Food 17% in Figure 2 and 12,950 for age 1 in Table 10, against 18% and 12,960 printed.
- `share_fit = to_100` fits Figure 2: the shares rounded on their own sum to 99 and give Housing 28% (28.355%); with the largest share taking the difference, Housing is 29% and every share matches. The report doesn't state this; it is the only reading here that gives the printed 29%.
- `cp_basis`: Table 10 applies Cp "of a particular age", and the single-year values are not printed, so `single` prints the hole. `group` (each age gets its group's Table 1 average) matches the printed column at ages 0 and 1 only. Not resolved.
- The lowest and highest income blocks' age-group rows are not transcribed in the case, so every figure for them prints a hole. The case quotes only their Total expenses rows, which are the asked figures themselves.
