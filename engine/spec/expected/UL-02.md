# UL-02 expected

Model: `tmp/design/models/UL-02.tally`. The model has two axes:
- `ul02_mode` (down, half-even, half-up) is the unstated rounding of the contribution.
- `ul02_mw_day` is which minimum wage the floor is taken from. `cy_start` is the law's reading: the wage in force on the first day of the contribution year. `in_month` is the trap the case describes: the wage in force in the month itself.

The published figures are the `cy_start` reading.

The rows are known from their promulgation: the 9% rate from 2021-11-23 (Dz.U. 2021 poz. 2105), the 2025 minimum wage from 2024-09-13, the 75% floor from 2024-12-23 and the 2026 minimum wage from 2025-09-15. The brochure's Tabela 4 shares are known from 2026-02-28. Every ask runs at the model's `on`, 2026-10-18, so it sees them all.

## ask jan_2026: `floor_base[2026-01]`, `min_contribution[2026-01]`

| Item | Case locator | Figure as stated | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| floor_base[2026-01] | ZUS page, scale/liniowy group, first bullet; brochure p. 7 section 2 | base 3.499,50 zł | ul02_mw_day = cy_start: 75% of 4666 (the minimum wage on 2025-02-01). Any mode. The rule is `base v dz2024_1915` |
| min_contribution[2026-01] | ZUS page, first bullet; brochure p. 7 Tabela 5 row 2025 | 314,96 zł | cy_start with half-up or half-even. Down gives 314,95 |

Under `in_month`, the floor is 75% of 4806 = 3604,50 and the contribution is 324,41 (half-up) or 324,40 (half-even or down). No source uses this reading.

```

$ python3 -c "from decimal import Decimal as D; print(D('3604.50')*D('0.09'))"
324.4050
```
