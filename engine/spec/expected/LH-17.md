# LH-17 expected figures

Model: `tmp/design/models/LH-17.tally`. Case file: `tmp/verification/cases/LH-17.md`. Each locator below is a front-matter entry of the case, given by its `name` with its `locator` in brackets. The case's `## Published answer` section repeats them in the paper's order.

**Round 2.** No figure changes. The `rp2000_exact` bound stays, now justified as a computation the language can't do, a numerical integral over a mortality table (§5.17, which names this case). A result that reaches it prints `bound: rp2000_exact: <reason>`, with the reason in the model; `base_gap` reads it inside arithmetic, which §5.16 allows. `eventual`'s base rule now points at an empty version `kalas2014_v`, so the Kalaš slides are cited (§5.3). `mr2005_v` is now known from the article's own date, 2005-11-01, not the archive capture (§4.5); every ask's cut is the model's `on`, so no figure moves.

## How the figures are read

**Probabilities print as fractions with ≈.** They are a gamma or normal CDF, an `exp` or an `ln`, correctly rounded to 1e-9. The source prints percents at varying precision: mostly 1 dp, sometimes 2 dp (4.27, 10.27, 12.27, 9.32, 0.52) or whole percents in the text. Each figure matches when the model's value, times 100, rounds half-up to the printed digits. The source states no rounding mode (case: Missing facts, third bullet).

**Axes.**
- `gamma_form`: `scale_beta` is the reading of Eq. 5 and of Kalaš slide 16, and it is the one the figures assume. `rate_beta` is the alternative the case names (case: Missing facts, "Gamma parameterisation"). It is reached by every gamma figure.
- `lam_round`: `exact` or `four_dp`. The case doesn't say which holds. It is reached by every figure with a finite lifetime, but not by the endowment (`lam[endow]` is the given 0). The printed figures carry too few digits to tell the two apart unless an engine shows otherwise.

The gamma CDFs can't be checked with one-line Decimal arithmetic (case: Transcription notes, "The gamma CDF itself was not evaluated"), so the ruin figures below are the published ones with no derivation.

**D7 (2026-10-19).** Both D7 evaluators computed the gamma CDFs (as Decimal series in long one-liners, a deviation in log/sessions.md) and agree on every cell (smoke/LH-17-a.md, smoke/LH-17-b.md § 2.5). Compared by the lead at each figure's printed digits:
- `scale_beta` with `lam_round = exact` reproduces every figure of the text and of Tables 2 and 3 but one: Table 2, age 70, $2.0 prints 1.61 where the model gives 0.018090946 (1.81). tmp/verification/checks/LH-17.md records it as a printed slip.
- `four_dp` misses 30 table cells (for example t2_a50 at $3.0, 10.25 against 10.27) and erg_90k (48.2 against 48.3). The check in tmp/verification counts two cells that rounding λ moves; D7's values move more. Either way the printed figures rest on unrounded λ.
- `rate_beta` reproduces none: every ruin is below 1.3e-7, and every solve is `RUN-SOLVE`.
- The solves (ss_10, ss_5, t5a, t5b) all match under `scale_beta`, under both λ readings.

- **`t2_endow` and `t2_endow_exact`.** Both are eventual ruin (λ = 0). The first comes from Eq. 8 and the second from Kalaš's form, `eventual`. They are the same function, so `t2_endow_diff` is 0 under both `gamma_form` options. It prints `≈ 0` with bound 1e-9 (D7, gaps/D7.md J54): §7.3 widens each rounded cell for the bound and doesn't see that the two are equal. At 1 dp it is the printed 0.0.
- **`t2_lam`.** It prints λ as a fraction: 0.0247 … 0.0937 under `four_dp`, or the unrounded values under `exact`. Times 100 and at 2 dp both give the printed column (derivation 3).
- **`t2_geo` and `t3_geo`.** They give μ − σ²/2 exactly: 0.05 and 0.0428 (derivation 5).
