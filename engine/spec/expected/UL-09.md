**Round 2.** No figure changes.
- **Old method.** The 2022 old method is now the marginal band `old_rate` (`marginal(old_rate, grondslag_p)`, LANGUAGE §5.8), not a sum over a band index.
- **Known dates.** The law rows are known from the publication of the act or regulation that enacted them (Staatsblad or Staatscourant), not from the consolidated text. The examples' facts and the modeller's rows are known from the model's `on`.
- **What each 2022 ask reads.** The Wet rechtsherstel row is read as Stcrt. 2023, 7968 set it (`law_wrh_reg2023`: 0,00% and 2,28%). It beats the act as published (`law_wrh2022`: 0,01% and 2,46%) by its later known date.

- **Cut.** Every ask's cut is `on`, so it sees every version.

**Readings used throughout.** The page's figures are reproduced on the axis line `ul09_method = page`, with `ul09_share = down` (the share is cut, not rounded, although the text says "Rond af"), `ul09_euro = down` (returns on bezittingen, the voordeel and the tax cut to whole euros) and `ul09_debt = half-up` (the debt return to the nearest euro). The column "Reading" names only the axes the row's figure depends on. An axis not named prints `*` on that row, or gives the same figure under each option. The register under each row lists these assumptions:
- `assumed: partner_all_year` on a row of a partner example whose figure passes through `npers` (the drempel, the heffingvrij vermogen, the green exemption and everything computed from them);
