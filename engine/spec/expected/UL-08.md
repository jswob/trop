# UL-08 expected

Model: `tmp/design/models/UL-08.tally`. Every locator is 26 CFR 31.3402(g)-1. T.D. 9276 (71 FR 42056-42057) prints the same figures.

Known dates (§4.5): the $1,000,000 threshold and the $100,000 agent floor are known from T.D. 9276's publication in the Federal Register, 25 July 2006, and govern from its effective date, 1 January 2007. The examples' stipulations, the 2007 rates included, are known from the model's `on`, 2026-10-18. Every ask runs at `on`, so it sees both versions. The withholding defs read the rates at the first day of the calendar year of payment, 2007-01-01; every payment here is in 2007, so no figure from Examples 1 and 3 changes from round 1.

The model has four axes:
- `ul08_route` is how the part below the threshold is withheld. `optional` is (a)(7)(iii)(F), at 25%; `aggregate` is (a)(6), which is a hole.
- `ul08_excess` is `portion` (mandatory withholding on the excess only) or `entire` ((a)(4)(iv), the whole payment).
- `ul08_agent` is the (a)(4)(iii) choice for an agent paying less than $100,000: `disregard` or `include`.
- `ul08_cents` is the rounding of the Example 4 gross-up to cents.

The examples print figures under `optional` and `portion`. Unless a row says otherwise, each figure below is that reading, under either `ul08_agent` and any `ul08_cents`. Under `aggregate`, every `wh_rest` with a part below the threshold is a hole, and so is its `wh_total`.

The solve pins the one cell `amount[e4_j]`, Example 4's payment, which has a hole row with the question "What gross payment to B leaves the $1,000,000 net bonus after withholding?
