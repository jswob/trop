## Readings

- **`mc_round`** is the only axis. ifirma's figures are `gr_half_up` and wFirma's are `gr_down`; they differ only for M2. Under `gr_exact` the relief prints as a fraction: 2030/17 for M1, 8408/17 for M2, 8710/17 for M3.
- **Monthly examples** run on current knowledge, in months the relief still covers. M1 and M2 are January 2022 (ifirma: "w miesiącu styczniu 2022 roku"). M3, M4 and Q1 give no month. M3 says "dla stawki podatku 17%", and the 17% advance rate held until 30 June 2022 (poz. 1265 art. 23 ust. 1), so their asks run January to June 2022, and every month gives the same line.
- **Annual answers** (Q2-Q7, S3) and the MF simulations (S1-S3) run `as known 2022-04-06`, the date the MF guides state the law at ("stan na", "wersja z" 06.04.2022). The employer guide itself is now known from 2022-04-07, the day it was put online; only the add-back rule cites it, and no `as known` ask reads that rule. Under current knowledge `ask_annual_now` gives false everywhere, by art. 18 ust. 2 of poz. 1265.
- **Knowledge of the examples.** The model now has `start 2022-01-01`. The examples' facts are stipulations, and the assumed rows that stand for their facts (an unlisted revenue is none; Q2's business A) go with them (§4.5): `ifirma_examples`, `mf_employer_examples`, `mf_taxpayer_examples` and `example_assumptions` are all known from 2022-01-01, so the `as known 2022-04-06` asks see them. `mf_employer_2022` (2022-04-07) has no items; the add-back rule cites it.
- `a_basis[…, joint]` is half the spouses' combined A (art. 26 ust. 4c). Pensions, a renta and an umowa zlecenia are not in A.
- **Bands** now start at `-inf` (§5.8), so an A below the lower limit, a loss included, is `below`. No example reaches a negative A, so no value moved.
- **No figure turns on the `wording` pin.** The MF answers are the law as it stood on 6 April 2022, before poz. 1265 was known, which `as known 2022-04-06` already gives; a `wording 2022-04-06` pin gives the same values, since poz. 1265 entered into force on 1 July 2022. `dz2022_1265_y` now records that day as `enters 2022-07-01` (its default would be 2022-06-15); no ask reads it.
- **The simulations are steps now, not bounds** (§5.17: a missing figure is a hole). The advance is `adv` (art. 32 ust. 1 pkt 1, ust. 2a, ust. 3) and the year's tax is `tax_ind` (art. 27 ust. 1), with the relief inside. Round 3 splits each into steps so that the rounding to full złoty is its own rule, citing `dz2011_1707`, the act that gave Ordynacja podatkowa art. 63 § 1 its wording: `adv_income` → `adv_base` (rounded) → `adv_raw` → `adv` (rounded), and `tax_income` → `tax_base` (rounded) → `tax_raw` → `tax_ind` (rounded). The arithmetic is the same as in round 2. Their missing figures are hole rows: the month's and the year's income (the costs and the social insurance contributions are not stated) and the PIT-2 statement. The 2021 law (`adv_2021`, `tax_2021_ind`, `tax_2021_joint`) and the joint method of art. 6 ust. 2 (`tax_joint`) are stubs, hole rules. "Without relief" is the same value `as if {ann_relief[…] = 0}` or `as if {mon_relief[…] = 0}`. Every simulation figure therefore prints `?` with its questions; the relief and A inside them print values.

## A check outside the sources

This is not part of the expected values. Nothing in the case gives these figures, so the model doesn't hold them. Assume the usual 2022 payroll figures: social insurance contributions of 13,71% of revenue, basic costs of 250 zł a month (3000 zł a year), and a PIT-2 statement on file. Filling the holes with them, the model's steps give every published figure but one. The columns are:
- S1: the salary, the advance with the relief, and without it;
- S2: the year's revenue, the tax with the relief, and without it;
- S3: each spouse's tax;
- S3 joint, read as twice the tax on half the joint income: the joint income, the tax with the relief, and without it.
