# MP-05 expected: IHT on gifts within seven years

Case file: `tmp/verification/cases/MP-05.md`. Model: `tmp/design/models/MP-05.tally`. Locators name the example under "Published answer" and the `published_answers` entry.

**Axis `mp05_period`**
- `printed`: the years and months the guidance prints.
- `dates`: completed years, and months, worked from the printed dates.

The case asks for both readings ("Missing facts and ambiguities", John bullet). An ask whose result is the same on both options prints `*`.

**Law dates.** The deaths are in 2012 and 2021, and the defs that read the band and the rate run at the date of death (`at`). The s. 7 rows, the taper band and the s. 7, s. 102 and s. 131 rules are in `fa1986` (known 1986-07-25, governing from 1986-03-18), and the rate and band in `fa2006` (known 2006-07-19, governing from 2009-04-06). Both govern every date the examples read. `ihtm_method` is now known 2019-05-31: the manual is guidance, its first release wasn't found, and 2019 captures show the rule's text under the manual-wide stamp of 31 May 2019 (§4.5). The hypothetical examples are known 2026-10-19. Every version is known by the cut, so every figure is as in round 1. A taper at seven years or more reads the band's hole row, but no example reaches it: `chargeable` excludes such a gift first.

Round 3 changes two things, and neither moves a figure:
- `running_before` no longer leaves the gift out by key. The gift's date compared with itself is decided false (§4.4), so the gift drops out as before, the month-dated Wayne gifts included.
- A gift made without a reservation has `reservation_end` `-` (assumed), and `pet_date.reserved` applies where it isn't `-` (parse rule 4). Only John's gift has a date, so `john_start` is as before.
