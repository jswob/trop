The model has two axes:
- `lh07_age`, which age a year's divisor uses:
  - `distribution`: age as of the birthday in the distribution year, which is the rule on p. 8 and in 1.401(a)(9)-5(c)(1);
  - `valuation`: age as of the birthday in the year before, which is how the Justin example prints its 2026 figure.
