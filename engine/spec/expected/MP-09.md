## The two rounding readings

The exact shares in cents, from the case's own quotients 439,00 × 300 : 1.224 and so on:

`python3 -c "from fractions import Fraction as F; t=F(300); w=[F('439.00'),F('428.50'),F('356.50')]; print([float(t*x/sum(w)*100) for x in w])"` → `[10759.803921568628, 10502.450980392157, 8737.745098039215]`

- `nearest` (half up, each on its own): 10760, 10502, 8738 cents, so 107,60, 105,02 and 87,38.
- `largest_rem`: rounded down, 10759 + 10502 + 8737 = 29998 cents, so 2 cents are left over. They go to the two largest remainders, K1 (0.80) and K3 (0.75), which gives 107,60, 105,02 and 87,38 again.

The case's transcription notes say the same about half up ("That rounding is my observation; the source does not state it").
