# UL-03 expected

Model: `tmp/design/models/UL-03.tally`. It has five axes:
- `ul03_repay`: the two FR wordings of the repayment, and (D8) `statute`, the statute's own repayment part with the whole premium rounded once.
- `ul03_round_at`: whether the 10-cent rounding comes before or after the repayment is added.
- `ul03_tie`: the tie rule of "nearest multiple of 10 cents". None of these figures has an exact tie, so it prints `*`.
- `ul03_mfs`: the top edge for married filing separately, statute or CFR. It keys the band `irmaa_mfs`, one table per reading.
- `ul03_tie1000`: the tie rule of "nearest multiple of $1,000" for the CPI-indexed edges. Only `edges_from_cpi` reaches it, and that ask is a hole.

The published figures match `ul03_repay = respectively`, with either `ul03_round_at` and any `ul03_tie`. `additional` adds $0.20 to every high-income total. `statute` reads 42 U.S.C. 1395r(i)(3)(A)(ii)(II) (Pub. L. 114-74 § 601(c)(3)): the IRMAA is (pct − 25 points) × (200 % × $405.40 + 4 × $0.20), so the repayment part is $0.28, $0.40, $0.52, $0.64 or $0.68, and the whole premium is rounded once; `ul03_round_at` doesn't reach it. It matches the print at every tier but t4, where it gives the single rounding `errata.md` l.42 suspects, $446.40 / $649.30 (D8).

The statute's rows are known from the acts that enacted them: the 200 percent and the 25 points from 2003-12-08 (Pub. L. 108-173), the 4 times the (a)(6) repayment from 2015-11-02 (Pub. L. 114-74), the 2019 table's percentages and base amounts from 2018-02-09 (Pub. L. 115-123). The CMS tables are known from 2025-11-14, the CFR reading's MFS table from the same day, and the FR rates from 2025-11-19. Every ask runs at the model's `on`, 2026-10-18, so it sees them all.
