## The two rounding readings

`python3 -c "from fractions import Fraction as F; print(F(10000)*5000/14000, F(10000)*3000/14000)"` → `25000/7 15000/7`

- `nearest`: 3571.43 rounds to 3,571, and 2142.86 rounds to 2,143.

The 3 leftover dollars go to the three largest remainders: B, C and D (.857 each) ahead of A (.429). That gives 3,571, 2,143, 2,143 and 2,143, the same as `nearest`.
