**In one sentence:** the engine now replays the plan against the records. Observations are booked
at the cut as the past, readings set balances as anchors or pads, a movement written on two
statements counts once, `bal(x, d) modelled` and the residual ledger set the model against the
records line by line, and fence F6 continues a projection's walk read again within one
timeline; the 26 checks of § M2, the goldens and pins on the prototype's bank history, and the
hindcasts H1–H5, H8, H9 print refcore's bytes.
